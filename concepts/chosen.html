About

I've spent nine years on software people use for hours at a stretch: dispatch boards, claims review, inventory reconciliation. The work is unglamorous, and the details carry it — a column 40px too narrow costs someone a hundred small decisions a day.

I spend the first week with whoever actually does the job, prototype in the second, and ship in small pieces after that. I write my own specs and stay until the thing is running in production; a handoff isn't a deliverable.
